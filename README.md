Invasion in Narandiba is a survival horror game where you must face a horde of zombies to reach the safe zone... Good luck!

//Enemy Mechanics//
The game's enemies are zombies that, when the player gets close, chase and attack mercilessly!
Even if one zombie isn't a big threat, be careful because they are never alone.

//SUPER WARRIOR//
Through a mutation in the zombie virus, the player can transform into a giant creature by attacking zombies a few times.
In this state, the player attacks a larger area and deals more damage, but due to their size, they are slower.

//Objective//
The objective of the game is to find the safe zone and eliminate all the zombies guarding the entrance.
The map has several paths to explore, containing secrets and mysteries on every street.
